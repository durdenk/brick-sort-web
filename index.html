How to play

Easy to learn.
Fun to plan.

Every level is a little 3D model. Your job is to take it apart without running out of room.

1

Spin the model

Drag to turn the model and see which bricks are free to take.

2

Pick a cup

Tap a cup to put it in a slot. Every free brick of its color flies straight in.

3

Keep slots free

A full cup pops and frees its slot. Fill every slot with cups that cannot fill, and you are stuck.

+

New twists along the way. Linked boxes, for example, travel in pairs: both must be at the front, and you need two free slots to take them.

Boosts

A little help
when you need it.

Stuck on a tricky model? Four boosts can turn a level around.

Extra Slot

Opens one more slot for the rest of the level.

Magnet

Pulls a waiting cup from the back of the line into an empty slot.

Lift

Moves a cup up out of its slot. The slot is free again, and the cup keeps collecting.

Vacuum

Fills a cup at once, even with bricks that are still covered.

Finish levels to earn coins, then spend them on boosts.

Good to know

Brick Color Sort is a single-player puzzle, designed for portrait play on Android phones. Your progress is saved on your device.
